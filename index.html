About me

I’ve always been drawn to the way things look, feel, and flow on a screen. Long before I studied computer science, I was the kid who spent hours reskinning apps like TeamSpeak, swapping out CSS, tweaking layouts, and learning how small visual changes could completely shift the experience. That early curiosity eventually grew into a degree, where my senior research thesis centered on data visualization in modern browsers, exploring how design can make complex information feel clear and engaging. What followed has been a career in software engineering, where I’ve worked as a full‑stack developer with a growing passion for UI and UX design. Outside of tech, I love camping, hiking, and gaming — the kinds of experiences that remind me how important it is for digital products to feel intuitive, inviting, and human. That perspective shapes the way I build: I’m drawn to modern, clean, mobile‑friendly interfaces that guide attention, encourage interaction, and stay accessible to everyone.

Design Influences

I draw from product and editorial design leaders; below are the specific qualities I take from each and how they shape my approach.

  • Apple: System-driven clarity and refined motion. I favor clear hierarchy, typographic precision, and subtle transitions that communicate purpose and affordance. Apple HIG
  • Spotify: Product-led empathy and expressive micro-interactions. I prioritize rhythm, discoverability, and personality in interfaces while keeping usability central. Spotify Design
  • Oura: Minimalism and a calm, premium feel. I reduce visual noise, emphasize breathing room, and use restraint to communicate value and calm focus. Oura — Horizon
  • Stripe: Interactive clarity and modular UI. I design components that are composable, predictable, and explicit about state—making complex flows feel straightforward. Stripe Elements, Stripe Design
  • Linear: Dark, precise productivity aesthetics. I take cues for focused surfaces, concise information density, and tooling that feels fast and deterministic. Linear — Design, Linear 2022
  • New York Times: Editorial clarity and narrative flow. I structure content with clear hierarchy and rhythm so users can scan, consume, and retain information efficiently. NYT Open, Designing at the Speed of News
  • Vox Media: Bold visual storytelling and data-driven presentations. I apply striking visuals and clear data layouts to support stories without sacrificing legibility. Vox Product, Vox Features
